# Naruto (ナルト)

**Genre:** Anime, Action, Adventure, Fantasy

## Overview
*Naruto* follows the story of **Naruto Uzumaki**, a young ninja who seeks recognition from his peers and dreams of becoming the **Hokage**, the leader of his village.

---

## Favorite Characters
* **Naruto Uzumaki** - The main protagonist with an unstoppable work ethic.
* **Kakashi Hatake** - The cool and skilled leader of Team 7.
* **Itachi Uchiha** - A legendary shinobi with a deep and complex story.

---

## Memorable Quote
> *"If you don't like your destiny, don't accept it. Instead, have the courage to change it the way you want it to be!"* — Naruto Uzumaki
